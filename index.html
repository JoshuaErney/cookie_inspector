🍪 Chrome Extension

Inspect, manage, and understand your browser cookies.

A clean, offline-first Chrome extension that opens on the site you are viewing, decodes JSON, JWT and Base64 values for you, and gives you full control of your cookies.

Get started

Up and running in three steps.

No account, no setup, no configuration.

  1. Install
    Add Cookie Inspector from the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Pin it
    Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar, then the pin next to Cookie Inspector.
  3. Open a site, click the icon
    It opens on that site's cookies. Click All sites to see every cookie in the browser.
Features

Everything you need to manage cookies.

A clear, live view of your cookies, with the tools to inspect, edit and control them.

🌐
This site / All sites
Opens on the cookies the site in your active tab can use: domain cookies for it and its parent domains, plus host-only cookies for that exact host. One click shows every cookie in the browser. The list updates live as sites set or remove cookies.
This site All sites Live updates
🔍
Search, filter & sort
Search by name, domain or value. Filter to Session, No HttpOnly, Insecure or Duplicate cookies. Sort domain groups by name, expiry or cookie count.
📋
Detail panel & auto-decode
Expand any cookie for its value, path, Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite, expiry, host-only status and partition. Values that can be decoded are decoded for you. JWT signatures are not verified.
JSON JWT URL-encoded Base64
✏️
Create cookies
Fill in a form with every attribute, or paste a cookie object, an array, or Base64-encoded JSON to create many at once. Chrome's rules are checked first, with a clear message when something is wrong.
Manual form JSON paste Base64 paste
🛡️
Safe editing
Edit any cookie in place. The new cookie is written before the old one is removed, so a change Chrome rejects never loses the original.
🔒
Security indicators
Cookies that page JavaScript can read (no HttpOnly) get an amber bar, which is worth a look on session and login cookies. Cookies without Secure have their own filter, and names repeated across domains are flagged as duplicates.
No HttpOnly Insecure Duplicate
🧹
Bulk actions
Clear every cookie for a domain, or make all of a domain's cookies session-only, in one click. There is no undo, so take a snapshot or export first.
📌
Domain pinning
Pin any domain to keep it at the top of the list, whatever the sort. Pins are saved across sessions.
🗄️
Snapshots
Save up to 10 named copies of your cookies. Restore sets them all again, overwriting matching cookies. It does not delete cookies created since, so it is not a full rollback.
📤
Export & import
Export the cookies in the current scope, one site or all, to a timestamped JSON file, and import a file back. If Chrome rejects some cookies, you are told how many.
🧩
Partitioned cookies
Partitioned (CHIPS) cookies are listed with a label and keep their partition when you edit them.
🌙
Dark mode
Follows your system's light or dark setting. Switch manually at any time; your choice is remembered.

Good to know

Straight answers.

What it does, and what it does not.

Does it send my cookies anywhere?

No. Cookie Inspector makes no network requests, has no analytics, and keeps its data in your browser. The font is bundled in the extension.

Why am I only seeing one site's cookies?

When the active tab is a website, Cookie Inspector opens on This site. Click All sites at the top to see every cookie. A host-only cookie on a parent or sibling host, such as example.com when you are on www.example.com, only appears under All sites.

Can I undo a delete?

No. Delete, Clear Domain and Make Session-Only act immediately. Take a snapshot or export the cookies first if you might want them back. Clear Domain removes every cookie stored for that domain, including ones hidden by a search or filter.

Does Restore roll everything back?

No. Restore sets every cookie from the snapshot again and overwrites matching ones, but it does not delete cookies that were created after the snapshot. A snapshot always contains every cookie in the browser, not just the current site.

Which browsers does it work in?

Cookie Inspector is built for Google Chrome (Manifest V3). It works on your normal browsing profile; incognito windows are not specifically supported.

Privacy Policy

Your data stays on your device.

Cookie Inspector is built to be entirely offline. No data is ever collected, transmitted, or shared.

Cookie Inspector does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

What Cookie Inspector does

Cookie Inspector is a Chrome browser extension that lets you view, inspect, and manage the cookies stored in your browser. It reads cookie data already present on your device and displays it within the extension popup. When you open the popup it also reads the address of your active tab, so it can show that site's cookies first. The address is used only inside the popup while it is open and is never saved or sent anywhere.

Data collection

We do not collect any data. Specifically:

  • No cookie names, values, or metadata are ever sent to any server
  • No browsing history or activity is recorded
  • No analytics or tracking of any kind is used
  • No third-party services are contacted

Local storage

Cookie Inspector uses Chrome's local storage API solely to save your theme preference (light or dark mode), any cookie snapshots you choose to create, and any domains you choose to pin. This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time by removing the extension.

Permissions

The extension requests the following Chrome permissions:

  • cookies — required to read, modify, and delete cookies on your behalf
  • storage — required to save your theme preference, snapshots, and pinned domains locally
  • host permissions (<all_urls>) — required so the cookies API can operate across all domains, and so the popup can read the active tab's address to choose which site's cookies to show first

These permissions are used exclusively to provide the features of the extension and for no other purpose.
